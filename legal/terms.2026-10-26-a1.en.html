Public gaming profile

Each account has an automatic gaming name and handle and a persistent Arcade avatar selected from a fixed gallery. You cannot upload files, edit a name or handle, or publish a BIO. Google names and photos are not published as gaming identity in this release. Legacy custom identity fields are normalized; old changed profile links may stop working. The public profile contains only gaming identity, avatar, total XP and level; email, account UUID, wallet and game records are private.

Public XP leaderboard

Authenticated accounts participate in the public XP leaderboard by default. The leaderboard may display rank, handle, display name, avatar, XP gained for today, this week, or all time, level, and a public-profile link. Guests do not participate. You can opt out in Settings; opting out removes the account from leaderboard results and rank calculations but does not make its public profile private.

Poker chips

Online CH and device-local Offline Chips are separate virtual game units with no cash value, cash redemption or real-money rewards. Offline progress is stored only on this device; clearing app data removes it. Offline play requires no account or Terms acceptance receipt.
