    Arcade Hub and Arcade Poker Privacy Policy

    Version: 2026-11-12-a3

    Controller and contact

    Data controller: Krzysztof Cal. KCSWH is the brand of a hobby project offering the free Arcade Hub and Arcade Poker services. Contact for the project and personal data: contact@kcswh.pl.

    Data we process

    We process your email address, account ID, authentication and session data, player profiles, XP, game records, virtual-unit settlements and technical data such as IP addresses and error information. Supabase provides authentication; passwords are not stored in plain text.

    If you choose to sign in with Google, Supabase receives identification and basic Google account information, including a name and photo. We do not receive your Google password. The Google name and photo are not automatically shown in your player profile. Any publication of a photo requires separate, informed permission.

    An account and an automatically generated profile may be created during registration, before you acknowledge the Terms. Profiles are available to signed-in users by default: after signing in, they can see the player name, handle, catalog avatar, XP and level. Leaderboard data is available after signing in. Accounts appear in the leaderboard by default. Opting out of the leaderboard does not hide the public profile. It does not display email, real name, sign-in credentials, balances or private game records. At a table, names and avatars help participants recognize each other. The website profile is also available to signed-in people who are not taking part in that game.

    Purposes and legal grounds

    • Account creation at your request, sign-in and necessary game and account features: GDPR Article 6(1)(b), where necessary to perform a contract for the service or take necessary steps at your request before entering into it. This legal ground does not depend solely on later acceptance of the Terms.
    • Public player identity and the leaderboard: Article 6(1)(f), the interest in recognizing participants and comparing players’ results. Acknowledging the Terms is not consent to data publication.
    • Security, abuse prevention, settlement integrity and keeping basic records of acceptance of the rules: Article 6(1)(f), protecting users and proper project operation.
    • Handling data rights: Article 6(1)(c), to meet the controller's legal obligations.
    • Optional website analytics: separate consent under Article 6(1)(a). You can withdraw it in the consent manager without affecting earlier lawful processing based on consent.

    Without data necessary for an account, account features cannot be used. Private guest play against bots and Poker Offline do not require an account.

    Providers and transfers

    Netlify hosts the website and API, Supabase provides authentication, the database and file storage, and Upstash supports game and XP data. We also use server-hosting and email services. Google participates in chosen sign-in and, after consent, website analytics.

    Processing may take place outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. The Supabase database region is in France; this does not mean all provider operations stay in the EEA. Netlify and Upstash processing agreements provide for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers and standard contractual clauses in the specified circumstances. Supabase describes standard contractual clauses as safeguards for transfers to Singapore and the USA. Contact contact@kcswh.pl for information about safeguards and a copy. Current provider terms: Netlify, Supabase, Upstash.

    Arcade's own websites do not use AdSense. Google Analytics starts only after consent. The Android app does not include Analytics or AdSense scripts. External games may use separate services described when they are made available.

    Retention

    Data Period or retention criterion
    Account and profile Until account deletion is completed, allowing for security and settlement of ongoing games.
    Account Terms acceptance records For as long as the account exists, with no separate expiry. Account deletion includes the related acceptance records.
    Guest Terms acceptance records Valid for one hour. Expired records are currently deleted in batches during later acceptances of the Terms. Expiry does not mean physical deletion exactly one hour later.
    Sessions, limits and caches According to session validity, the expiry of each entry or cache replacement. Redis data does not all share one expiry period.
    Logs and recovery backups Until rotation or deletion under capacity limits, service scope and provider rules. Retention depends on the type of record and available plan; there is no single period for all these data. Deleting a database record does not immediately erase it from an existing backup.
    Game and settlement history, and completed account-deletion requests Settled game and settlement records, including their private archive and recovery copies, and the data of completed account-deletion requests, are automatically deleted or minimized once they are older than 12 months. Deletion runs periodically and in batches, so it may happen after this period ends and may take longer when there is a backlog. We keep only the following for longer: the balance history of an account that still exists, because it is needed to prove that balance; the settlement history of a deleted account as a financial record, without the profile or sign-in data; the identifier of a deleted account, so that it cannot be reused; records needed to finish a game or settlement in progress.
    File permissions and other technical account records Some records have no automatic deletion date and may remain after account deletion. Further retention needs a specific justification, such as checking a disputed settlement, resolving an outstanding incident or dispute, or meeting a legal obligation. The absence of automatic deletion alone does not justify keeping data indefinitely.
    Correspondence While handling the matter and preserving its history where needed for follow-up questions, a dispute or a legal obligation.

    An acknowledgement contains an identifier, version, server timestamp and request ID. Its validity does not determine retention of other data.

    Cookies and local storage support sessions, preferences and caches. Website consent choices have a 180-day validity. A remembered avatar may be used for Offline appearance, without synchronizing game progress. Signing out does not remove local saves; you can clear them through browser or app settings.

    Your rights and account deletion

    If an objection to profile visibility is upheld, the operator can block the profile from being shared with other signed-in users without deleting the account. This is not a switch in user settings. A profile block does not remove the identity needed by participants in an ongoing game; we consider any request about leaderboard visibility separately.

    You may request access, correction, erasure and restriction, and portability where applicable. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, including the public profile, through contact@kcswh.pl. Leaderboard opt-out is a separate setting and does not replace this right. Your request may also cover records remaining after account deletion. Reviewing it requires identifying which data is still needed, for what specific purpose and on what legal ground. Any refusal to erase data must be explained. Not every request entails deleting the entire settlement history or all files. We also consider requests concerning children's data.

    Request account deletion in Account when the feature is available, or by email. The process removes the profile and sign-in identity after safely handling game participation and the virtual balance. We do not promise immediate deletion of all logs, backups and integrity records. You do not need to accept new Terms to make a request or obtain support.

    We respond without undue delay, normally within one month. If an extension is necessary under the GDPR, we will explain it within the first month. We may request proportionate identity verification; do not send passwords or tokens. You may complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
