Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Who we are

Data controller: KCSWH, contact@kcswh.pl. This policy covers the Arcade Hub portal, its games, XP system, and optional Supabase-backed user accounts.

What data we collect

  • Email address for account login and communications.
  • Password hash stored only by Supabase; we never store raw passwords.
  • Session tokens and authentication cookies issued by Supabase.
  • Account metadata such as display name and avatar initials.
  • Public-profile data: automatic gaming handle and display name, chosen catalog avatar, Terms version and acceptance time.
  • XP and game progress tied to your user ID for cloud sync.
  • Security and activity logs (e.g., sign-in events, error diagnostics).
  • Consent and analytics data when you opt in via the consent manager.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in is optional. If you choose it, Google provides Supabase with your Google account identifier, verified email address and basic profile data, such as your name and profile image. These data are stored as part of Supabase authentication and used to create or link your Arcade Hub account, sign you in and maintain your session. We do not receive your Google password or access to Gmail, Drive or Calendar. The Supabase browser SDK stores session data and may retain a Google access token; the application does not copy or forward that token to its APIs. Gaming-profile visibility is described below; your email address and tokens are not public. You can request account deletion by emailing contact@kcswh.pl as described in the user-rights section.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Account creation, authentication, and email verification – Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract).
  • XP cloud sync and account management – Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Security, fraud prevention, service integrity – Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).
  • Compliance with legal obligations (e.g., security logging) – Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.
  • Analytics and optional measurements – Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent via the consent manager).

How we use your data

  • Provide and secure your account, including sign-in, sign-out, and session management.
  • Sync XP, achievements, and game progress across devices.
  • Send verification emails and required service notices.
  • Maintain security logs, detect abuse, and protect against fraud or cheating.
  • Run analytics only after you consent to statistics cookies.

Public gaming profiles

Accounts receive an automatic gaming name/handle and a permanent catalog avatar. You may choose a different catalog avatar. BIO, manual identity editing and file uploads are unavailable. Google identity data remain private authentication data; this release does not import Google photos. The public profile shows gaming identity, avatar, total XP and level, not email, account UUID, CH or game records. Legacy custom identity is normalized; changed old profile URLs may no longer work.

Terms records and local Offline data

We store the accepted Terms version, server timestamp and a request identifier linked to your account, to demonstrate acceptance and protect service integrity. Account receipts are removed with the account. Guest receipts expire after one hour and are removed by routine retention. Accepting Terms is separate from any future voluntary Google-photo consent. Old profile image objects are removed through the existing controlled retention process, not by publishing or reusing them. Offline Chips, cards and sessions stay on the device and are not synchronized with CH or XP.

Public XP leaderboard

Authenticated accounts are included in the public XP leaderboard by default. Leaderboard entries may show rank, profile handle, display name, avatar, XP for the selected period (today, this week, or all time), level, and a link to the public profile. Guest players are not included. The leaderboard does not display email addresses, Supabase user IDs, chip balances, ledger or poker data, session data, or detailed XP events.

You can hide your account from the leaderboard in Settings. This removes it from leaderboard results and rank calculations, but does not make the public profile at /u/<handle> private.

Data processing & Supabase notice

We use Supabase (Supabase Inc., 970 Toa Payoh North #07-04, Singapore; infrastructure in the EU and USA) as our data processor for authentication, database storage, and email verification. Supabase processes email, password hash, session tokens, account metadata, and XP records on our behalf. Data may be transferred to the USA; Supabase applies Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the safeguards described in their Data Processing Agreement. Supabase stores backups according to its retention policy and secures data with encryption in transit and at rest.

Third-party games and iframes

Some games may be loaded from external providers in iframes. These partners may act as independent controllers for their services and may process data in line with their own terms and privacy policies. When you use such games, please review the notices or links presented with them to understand how your data may be processed.

Cookies and similar technologies

  • Authentication cookies from Supabase are strictly necessary and cannot be turned off without disabling sign-in.
  • Preferences and consent cookies store your consent manager choices.
  • Analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics) load only after you provide consent through the consent manager.
  • You can revisit choices anytime via the Manage cookies control.

Data retention

  • Account data (email, metadata, XP, and public-profile data) is retained until you delete your account or request removal.
  • Security and diagnostic logs are kept for approximately 30–90 days unless required longer for safety or legal reasons.
  • Backups are retained per Supabase’s backup schedules.

International transfers

Supabase and other providers may process data outside your country, including in the USA. Transfers rely on SCCs and comparable safeguards.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to processing. You may withdraw consent for analytics at any time via the consent manager. To exercise rights, email contact@kcswh.pl. You may lodge a complaint with the Polish Data Protection Authority (UODO).

Account deletion

You can request account deletion in Account when the in-app feature is available. You can also request deletion or assistance by emailing contact@kcswh.pl. Requests follow the existing account-deletion process; restricted accounting or security evidence may be retained as required by the retention policy and applicable law.

Security

We apply industry-standard measures, use Supabase’s managed security controls, and limit access to personal data. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your device and credentials safe.

Links and related terms

See our Terms of Service and Cookies Notice for more details on service rules and cookie choices.
