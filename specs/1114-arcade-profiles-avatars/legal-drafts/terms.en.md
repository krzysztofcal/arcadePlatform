Krzysztof Cal runs a non-commercial hobby project under the KCSWH brand. Arcade Hub and Arcade Poker are the free services offered through the project. Contact: contact@kcswh.pl.

## 2. Using the games

The games are available free of charge. CH and Offline Chips are separate game units. They have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money and do not provide cash prizes.

Online play and account features require internet access. Private guest play against bots does not require an account. Poker Offline runs on your device; its progress and Offline Chips are not synchronized with online play. Clearing app or browser data may delete local saves.

The website requires a browser supporting JavaScript, cookies and local storage. Email registration requires access to your mailbox and email confirmation. You may also choose Google sign-in. Android app requirements are provided when the app is made available.

## 3. Accounts, profiles and accepting the rules

Registration creates an account. Signing in with Google for the first time may also create one. An account and automatically generated profile may be created before you accept the Terms. Signing in does not itself mean acceptance.

Player profiles are available to signed-in users by default. After signing in, they can see the automatically assigned player name and public handle, catalog avatar, XP and level. Your email and sign-in credentials are not part of the public profile. Leaderboard data is available after signing in. Accounts appear in the leaderboard by default; you can opt out in Account, but this does not hide the public profile. At a table, names and avatars help participants recognize each other.

When acceptance of the current Terms is required, you must accept the displayed version before starting a new game while signed in, changing your public avatar, or sending a reaction or gift. We record the version and confirmation time. Acceptance has no retroactive effect and is not consent to analytics or publication of a Google photo. “Not now” records no acceptance.

Even if you do not accept a new version, you can still read the documents, obtain support, sign out, request account deletion and change your leaderboard visibility. You can resume an existing game, leave the table and complete its settlement. Private guest play against bots and Offline do not require confirmation before every game.

## 4. Conduct

Do not impersonate others, harass players, distribute unlawful content, cheat or bypass safeguards. Reactions and gifts are visible during play. Do not share sign-in credentials with others.

Avatars are selected from a catalog. You cannot manually change your public player name or handle, add a profile description or upload your own photo. Your Google account name and photo are not automatically published in your player profile. Any future use of a photo requires your separate, informed permission.

Report problems or inappropriate conduct to contact@kcswh.pl.

## 5. Leaving and complaints

You may stop using the project and sign out. Signing out does not delete your account or local game saves. Request account deletion in Account when the feature is available, or through contact@kcswh.pl. The procedure ends participation in games and handles the virtual balance; it does not reverse completed settlements. It does not require acceptance of new Terms.

Send complaints or bug reports to contact@kcswh.pl. Describe the problem, when it occurred and how to contact you. Do not send passwords, tokens or sign-in codes. Reports are considered individually and answered by email. GDPR time limits apply to personal-data requests.
